Visit DAKHLA

The magical city — your host for AISEC 2027

Why Visit Dakhla

The Saharan Pearl

Dakhla is one of Morocco's most captivating destinations, where the vast Sahara meets the Atlantic Ocean. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, rich Saharan heritage, and growing international appeal, the city offers a unique setting for an international conference bringing together innovation, technology, and culture.

The conference venue, ENCG Dakhla, part of Ibn Zohr University, provides a modern academic environment equipped with conference halls, lecture rooms, and dedicated facilities, making it an ideal setting for knowledge exchange and international collaboration.

Must-Visit Attractions

WHITE DUNES

a crescent-shaped pale sand formation rising from the turquoise waters of Dakhla Lagoon that shifts with the tides between reflective sandflats and a water-surrounded island home to flamingos and fiddler crabs.

IMLILI OASIS

a rare Saharan desert depression near Dakhla known for its natural waterholes and unique desert fish, while Dakhla's famous White Dune (Dune Blanche) is a separate white-sand dune along the lagoon often visited on the same desert excursion.

DRAGON ISLAND

a wild, uninhabited, dragon-shaped sand-and-rock isle in Dakhla's lagoon known for its desert-meets-ocean scenery, turquoise waters, clay baths, and migratory birdlife.

PUERTO RICO BEACH

a remote, uncrowded Atlantic cove featuring pale, fine sand, dramatic cliffs, and clear water near the Tropic of Cancer, popular for camping under the stars, swimming, and water sports.

FARO DE DAKHLA

a 50-meter cylindrical tower painted with horizontal white and black bands and topped with a black gallery and lantern, standing on the Arciprés Grande peninsula along the Atlantic coast near Dakhla.

SAHRAOUI VILLAGE

Sahraoui Village offers a traditional Sahrawi experience with desert tents, local music, storytelling, camel rides, tea, and regional dishes.
