AISEC CTF — Dakhla Edition

A national cybersecurity competition organized by Club GCDSTE (ENSA Marrakech) as part of AISEC, the International Summit on Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity.

The 2027 edition travels to the dunes of Dakhla, hosted within the celebration of Moroccan sovereignty over the Sahara. Hunt flags where the desert meets the Atlantic.
